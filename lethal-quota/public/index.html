LETHALQUOTA

A co-op scrap salvage horror game — with proximity voice chat.

How to play

You are a contracted employee of The Company. Land on abandoned moons, enter the facility, collect scrap and bring it back to the ship. Every 3 days you must sell enough at the Company to meet the profit quota — or you're fired.

Your microphone is proximity based: crewmates only hear you when they're near. Carry a walkie-talkie to talk across the map. Blind dogs outside can hear you talk. The dead can only talk to the dead.
